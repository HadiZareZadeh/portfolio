# Plan: Getting Qualified for the Austria Job Market

**Target:** Austria  
**Profile:** Data Science, ML/AI, full‑stack (Python, Vue, Laravel)  
**Goal:** Become qualified and job‑ready for Austrian tech roles (Data Science, ML, or Software Engineering).

---

## 1. Visa & residence (know the path first)

To work in Austria as a non‑EU national you typically need a **job offer** and a **residence title**. Main options:

| Route | Relevance to you | Notes |
|-------|------------------|--------|
| **Red‑White‑Red Card – Very Highly Qualified** | ✅ Best fit if you can reach 70 points | Points from: degree (max 40), experience (20), **language (15)**, age (20), previous salary (20). No labour market test. |
| **Red‑White‑Red Card – Other Key Workers** | ✅ Most common with employer | Job offer required. Min. salary ~€3,225 gross/month (2025). 55+ points; labour market test may apply. |
| **EU Blue Card** | ✅ If salary is high enough | For highly qualified employment; salary threshold applies (check current figure). |
| **Job‑seeker visa** | Optional | Allows a limited period in Austria to search for work; rules and duration vary—check current requirements. |

**Action items:**
- [ ] Read the official [Red‑White‑Red Card](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/) and points criteria (e.g. [workinaustria.com](https://www.workinaustria.com/en/residence-employment/point-scheme/)).
- [ ] Estimate your points (degree, years of experience, language, age) to see if you’re close to 70 (Very Highly Qualified) or 55+ (Other Key Workers).
- [ ] Decide whether to apply from abroad (with job offer) or explore a job‑seeker visa if you want to search from inside Austria.

## 2. Degree recognition (qualification proof)

Your B.Sc. from Iran is not automatically equivalent in Austria. For **non‑regulated** roles (e.g. Data Scientist, Software Developer), formal **nostrification** is often not mandatory, but employers may want clarity.

- **ENIC NARIC Austria** (via [aais.at](https://www.aais.at)): Request an **appraisal/validation** of your degree. This states equivalence to an Austrian degree and helps in job applications.
- **Nostrification**: Required only for regulated professions (e.g. certain engineers). If you ever need it, you apply to an Austrian university that has a comparable programme (e.g. TU Wien); they may ask for supplementary exams.

**Action items:**
- [ ] Submit an application to **ENIC NARIC Austria** for degree appraisal (documents: diploma, transcript, passport, etc.).
- [ ] Add the result to your CV and LinkedIn (e.g. “B.Sc. Computer Science, equivalent to Austrian university degree per ENIC NARIC Austria”).

---

## 3. Language (critical for points and employability)

- **English (B2):** Enough for many tech roles; international companies often work in English.
- **IELTS/TOEFL:** Useful for Red‑White‑Red points and for proving level to employers. Aim for **IELTS 6.5+** (or equivalent) and add it to your CV.
- **German:** Not mandatory for all IT jobs but **strongly recommended**. It increases points (Red‑White‑Red), opens more jobs, and helps daily life and integration.
  - Target: **B1** (conversational) as a first milestone, then **B2** for broader options.
  - Use: ÖSD/Goethe exams (recognised in Austria), plus apps, courses, or tutors.

**Action items:**
- [ ] Book and pass **IELTS** (or TOEFL); list score and date on CV and LinkedIn.
- [ ] Start **German** (A1 → A2 → B1). Set a deadline for B1 (e.g. within 12–18 months).
- [ ] On LinkedIn and CV, state: “English: B2 (IELTS …)”, “German: A2 (learning)” (update as you progress).

---

## 4. Skills & qualifications (what to strengthen)

Austria has demand in **R&D, STEM, and startups**; Data Science and ML roles exist, especially in larger firms and tech hubs (Vienna, Graz, etc.). To be clearly “qualified”:

- **Core (you already have):** Python, ML basics, data wrangling, Vue, Laravel, automation.
- **Strengthen for market:**
  - **PyTorch or TensorFlow:** Move from “basic” to “comfortable” (e.g. one end‑to‑end project: data → model → deployment or API).
  - **Cloud:** “Basic Google Cloud” → at least one cloud (GCP or AWS) with a small project (e.g. training/job on cloud, or simple API). Helps for “Very Highly Qualified” and modern job descriptions.
  - **SQL / data engineering:** Solid SQL and one pipeline or ETL-style project (even small) will match many “Data” job ads.
  - **Certifications (optional but useful):** e.g. AWS Cloud Practitioner or a Google Data/ML cert; or a focused ML course (Coursera, etc.) with a certificate. List them on LinkedIn and CV.

**Action items:**
- [ ] One **portfolio project** in PyTorch or TensorFlow (e.g. classification, NLP, or time series) and document it on GitHub + portfolio.
- [ ] One **cloud project** (e.g. train/serve a small model on GCP or AWS) and add to portfolio.
- [ ] Add 1–2 **certifications** (cloud or ML) and keep LinkedIn/cert platforms in sync.
- [ ] Ensure **resume** and **portfolio** use the same keywords as Austrian job ads (Data Science, Machine Learning, Python, etc.).

## 5. Job search (where to look)

- **Portals:** [Karriere.at](https://www.karriere.at), [StepStone.at](https://www.stepstone.at), [DevJobs.at](https://www.devjobs.at) (including “IT jobs without German”), [EnglishJobSearch.at](https://englishjobsearch.at).
- **LinkedIn:** Set location to “Austria”, follow Austrian tech companies and recruiters.
- **Companies:** Apply directly on career pages of Austrian/international firms (e.g. AIT, startups from Austrian Startup Monitor, banks, industry).
- **Startups:** Austrian startup ecosystem has deep‑tech and AI; check startup lists and incubators (e.g. AIT Austrian Startup Monitor).

**Action items:**
- [ ] Create/search alerts for “Data Scientist”, “Machine Learning”, “Python”, “Software Engineer” in Austria on 2–3 portals.
- [ ] Follow 10–20 Austrian tech companies and 5–10 recruiters on LinkedIn.
- [ ] Apply consistently (e.g. 5–10 quality applications per week) and track in a spreadsheet.

---

## 6. Application materials (CV & LinkedIn)

- **CV:** 1–2 pages; clear sections (Experience, Education, Skills, Projects). Optional: professional photo (common in DACH). State **work authorization** if relevant (e.g. “Eligible for Red‑White‑Red Card”).
- **LinkedIn:** Headline and “About” should mention Data Science / ML and that you’re targeting the Austrian market. Add ENIC NARIC result, IELTS, German level, and key projects.
- **Portfolio:** Keep this repo and live site up to date; add 1–2 new projects (ML + cloud) and link from LinkedIn and CV.

**Action items:**
- [ ] Rewrite CV for Austrian audience (English is fine for English‑language roles); add degree equivalence and language levels.
- [ ] Update LinkedIn headline, location (e.g. “Open to opportunities in Austria”), and skills.
- [ ] Add “Austria” or “Vienna” in LinkedIn job preferences.

---

## 7. Networking

- **LinkedIn:** Connect with recruiters and engineers in Austria; engage with their posts; ask for short calls or tips.
- **Events:** Online or in‑person tech/Data Science meetups (Vienna, Graz); check Meetup, Eventbrite, and company pages.
- **Chambers / institutions:** Awareness of **WKO** (Wirtschaftskammer) and **workinaustria.com** for general info; no need to join immediately.

**Action items:**
- [ ] Send 5–10 personalised connection requests per week to people in Austrian tech/Data Science.
- [ ] Join 2–3 Austria‑ or EU‑focused Data/ML groups on LinkedIn.
- [ ] Attend at least one webinar or meetup related to Data Science/ML in Austria or remote.

## 8. Timeline (suggested milestones)

| Phase | Focus | Rough timeline |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| **1. Foundation** | ENIC NARIC application, IELTS, German A1–A2, 1 ML project | Months 1–3 |

Adjust according to your current job, family, and how fast you can study German and complete projects.
